# ZEN Report 📊

A lightweight, browser-based pricing calculator for estimating the cost of ZEN Individual plans based on the published pricing document.

This project contains a single-page HTML tool that helps compare annual costs across ZEN's Good, Better, Best, and Zen plans, including optional services, transaction assumptions, card FX fees, cash withdrawals, and Reward Zone estimates.

## ✨ What this tool does

- Compares estimated yearly costs across ZEN plans
- Models common funding and transfer fees
- Includes FX, cash withdrawal, and ATM assumptions
- Evaluates optional Reward Zone redemption estimates
- Lets you adjust annual values, counts, and rate assumptions
- Shows a plan-by-plan breakdown for quick comparison

## 🧮 Included in the calculator

- Funding and top-up methods
- Transfers and local payment fees
- Currency exchange assumptions
- Cash and ATM costs
- Card-related charges and estimated benefits
- Account maintenance and exceptional-case scenarios
- Optional cashback and Reward Zone credit estimates

## 🚀 Quick start

1. Open `zen_pricing_calculator.html` in a browser.
2. Enter your expected annual values and transaction counts.
3. Adjust assumptions such as FX market-hour usage or Reward Zone redemption value.
4. Review the plan comparison and detailed cost breakdown.

## 📝 Notes

- The calculator is designed around the published ZEN Individual pricing PDF.
- Some values are estimates and should be verified against the current official pricing document.
- The app runs entirely client-side in the browser with no build step required.
