Model scope and notes

  • All monetary usage fields are entered as EUR-equivalent amounts. For percentage + fixed fees, cost = percentage of entered transfer/top-up volume + fixed fee per transaction.
  • ATM withdrawal fees assume the stated average monthly cash withdrawal repeats each month. The plan-free allowance is applied month by month.
  • Customer-ordered FX assumes exchange volume is spread evenly over the selected months, and applies the monthly free tier to that volume.
  • Card top-up rates can vary by card type, card-issuing country and top-up currency. The PDF says the actual rate is shown in-app; adjust those fields to match your quote.
  • Third-party ATM surcharges, sender/recipient bank fees outside ZEN's stated fee, exchange-rate movement, taxes and unlisted provider charges are not included.
  • Some listed “incidental costs” apply only to exceptional cases (for example, manual processing of funds after account closure or payments of blocked funds). They are opt-in inputs and default to zero.
